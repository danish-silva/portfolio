Led a seven member engineering team building an in-house EN 352 compliance testing system for Sensear's industrial earmuff headsets, replacing costly external lab testing, while owning project direction, task allocation and client engineering communication.

Designed the test system electronics end to end, including ESP32-based control, a high-torque servo drive for headband fatigue cycling, and a multi-rail power supply with buck and linear regulation plus reverse-polarity, over-current and over-voltage protection.

Engineered the headband force measurement front end, using a Wheatstone bridge load cell with a 24-bit ADC, differential filtering and ratiometric excitation, for repeatable clamp force measurement to EN 13819-1.

Delivered full system schematics, a custom ESP32-S3 PCB and a costed bill of materials, and specified the acoustic measurement chain (loudspeaker array and audio interface) for attenuation testing per ISO 4869.
